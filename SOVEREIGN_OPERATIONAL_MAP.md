## 🧠 2. The AI Brain Architecture: Level 18 Autonomy
The AI is composed of four distinct intelligence layers that work in perfect synchronicity.

### **Layer 1: The Learning Engine (Omega Protocol)**
The Learning Engine is the "Institutional Memory." It recursively crawls every sector of the database:
- **Financial Ingestion:** Reads every Invoice and Quote to calculate "Portfolio Yield."
- **Resource Ingestion:** Analyzes Staff and Equipment nodes to identify "Resource Contention."
- **Narrative Ingestion:** Reads the raw text of Site Diaries to calculate the "Friction Index" (identifying rework, delays, and morale drops).

### **Layer 2: The Sovereign Oracle (The Advisor)**
This is the front-facing AI Partner. It operates in the **War Room** and **Mission Deck**:
- **Persona Aware:** It knows your specific company niche via the **Company Bio**.
- **Site-Ready Voice:** It speaks in high-impact, direct English—no corporate jargon.
- **Predictive Mentorship:** It doesn't wait for you to ask; it pushes signals (e.g., "Yield Optimization Detected") to the Neural HQ.

### **Layer 3: The Agency Engine (The Agent)**
The Agency Engine is the AI's ability to **write back to reality**. It allows the AI to:
- **Self-Correct Pricing:** Automatically updates quote margins based on rising material costs.
- **Temporal Shifting:** Moves project dates in the database when a weather delay is detected.
- **DNA Re-writing:** Updates staff productivity rates based on their actual performance in the field.

### **Layer 4: The Audit Ultra Log (The Memory)**
A tamper-proof, immutable record of every action taken by humans or AI.
- **Transaction Identity:** Every change has a unique UUID.
- **Structural State Data:** Stores the "Before" and "After" of every database change in raw JSON.
- **Traceability:** Allows the user to reconstruct the entire history of a project or quote in seconds.

## 🏛️ 3. Feature Breakdown: The Sovereign Suite

### **1. The Neural HQ (Executive Command)**
A SpaceX-grade mission control for managing a multi-project empire.
- **Orbital Mesh:** A 3D visualizer where projects orbit your company core. Color-coded by profitability (Green = Profit, Red = Risk).
- **Portfolio Hub:** Live dashboard showing "Paid Capital" (Cash in bank) and "Net Margin" across the whole company.
- **Mission Deck:** A spacious, welcoming input area to ask your AI Partner anything about your business.
- **System Health Center:** Real-time indicators for the Database, AI Core, and Neural Mesh stability.

### **2. Neural Estimation (Quote Builder)**
The world's first "Visual Logic" estimation engine.
- **Area Nodes:** Drag and resize "Living Room" or "Exterior" nodes.
- **Recursive Linking:** Connect an Area to a Material. If the area changes, the material cost auto-calculates.
- **Profit Nodes:** A master node that controls markup, overhead, and contingency for the entire visual graph.
- **Estimation Prism:** A HUD that scans for "Zero Rate Risks" and "Coverage Gaps."

### **3. Neural Workflow (Workflow Architect)**
A visual programming language for construction logic.
- **The Time Machine:** Use a temporal slider to scrub the lattice backward and forward in time.
- **Lattice Branching:** Create "Scenario A" and "Scenario B" to plan for risks (e.g., "Heavy Rain Plan").
- **Predictive Ghosting:** The AI anticipates the next node you need and projects it as a shadow node. Click to manifest it into reality.

### **4. Paint Diary (The Execution Canvas)**

- **Smart Allowances:** Nodes that automatically apply bonuses (e.g., "Height Allowance") when linked to a worker.
