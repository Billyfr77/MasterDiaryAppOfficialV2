MasterDiaryOS is designed using a Recursive Intelligence Model. This means it doesn't just store
  data; it uses that data to rewrite its own "DNA."

  As you feed it more Quotes, Diaries, and Invoices, the app transitions from a simple tool into an
  Industrial Brain. Here is the step-by-step explanation of how this power compounds:

  ---

  1. The Sensor Array (How Data Enters)
  Every action in the app is a "Sensor" for the AI:
   * The Quote (The Plan): Tells the AI what you think will happen (e.g., "This slab should take 8 hours").
   * The Diary (The Reality): Tells the AI what actually happened (e.g., "The slab took 12 hours because the
     pump broke").
   * The Invoice (The Yield): Tells the AI the final financial truth (e.g., "We only got paid for 8 hours").

  2. The Neural Omega Processor (The "Learning")
  The Learning Engine we implemented recursively crawls these entries. It calculates two critical metrics
  that make the app "Sentient":
   * The Efficiency Index: It compares your Quoted Hours vs. Actual Hours. If you consistently take 20%
     longer on a specific task (like "Prep"), the AI notices this pattern.
   * The Friction Index: It reads the raw text in your site diaries. It looks for words like "broken,"
     "delayed," or "rework." It connects these words to your profit loss.

  3. The Strategic Feedback Loop (The "Power")
  This is where the app becomes more powerful. When you open the Quote Builder or the War Room for a new
  project, the AI is no longer using "General AI knowledge." It is using Your Company DNA:

   * Cold Start (No Data): The AI says: "Standard industry rate for painting is $50/hr."
   * Power State (100 Diaries Ingested): The AI says: "We know our specific crews actually take 1.2x longer
     than the industry standard on heritage buildings. I have automatically adjusted the labor DNA in this
     quote to protect our 25% margin."

  ---

  🚀 A Real-World Example of the Learning Loop:

   1. Month 1: You quote 10 projects. You estimate "Waste" at 5%.
   2. Month 2: Your site foremen log 50 diaries. They mention "running out of materials" 15 times.
   3. Month 3: The Neural Oracle detects this friction. It calculates that your "Actual Waste" is 12%.
   4. Month 4: When you go to create a new Quote, the AI Partner intervenes: "Based on our last 50 diaries,
      5% waste is causing delays. I've updated our Material DNA to 12% waste. This will cost us $2k more
      upfront but save us $8k in lost labor time."
